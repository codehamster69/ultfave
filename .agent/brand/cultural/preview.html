01 The mark

Three ideas, one fold.

A mark has to survive 16 pixels, one colour and a screen full of numerals. These were the three routes; only one earned the icon.

The Folded U

A crate-solid U with its top-right corner turned down like a folded sleeve. The gesture you leave on a record you love, and on the artist you keep coming back to.

Chosen / reads at 16px, one path, no cliche

The Groove

Concentric grooves with a needle cut. Instantly readable as vinyl, but the record-in-a-circle is the most borrowed music mark there is, and it drifts toward Spotify territory.

Rejected / category cliche, adjacency risk

The Tally

Stacked score bars climbing right. Competitive and obvious, which is the problem: it is the default leaderboard glyph and says nothing about music, fandom or this audience.

Rejected / generic leaderboard grammar

Display

Bricolage Grotesque

Variable optical size and width, slightly awkward terminals. Reads like a record label at 100px and stays legible at 28. Fallback: Archivo, Helvetica Neue.

Sans

Instrument Sans

Tall x-height, tight apertures, made for interfaces. Keeps the leaderboard calm under the display type. Fallback: Inter, system-ui.

Mono

JetBrains Mono

Tabular by default, unambiguous zero, good at 10px. Every score, rank, delta and timestamp. Fallback: SFMono, Menlo.

next/font/google keeps the existing --font-display, --font-sans, --font-mono variables. No other code changes.

Card rules

  • The numeral takes its rank colour — brass, periwinkle or rose. Coral only on your own card.
  • Artist name wins the frame. Rank is second; handle and stats are evidence.
  • Never fake a rank. Unranked renders an em dash and the word UNRANKED.
  • Wordmark top-left, domain bottom-right. That is the whole signature.

09 Rules

Keep it ownable.

The identity is only premium if it is applied with restraint. These are the lines that hold it together.

Do

  • Keep 90% of any screen in cellar and cream. Colour is a signal, not a surface.
  • Set every number in mono, tabular, right-aligned.
  • Use coral for one action per view. If two things are coral, one is wrong.
  • Let the mark breathe. Clear space is half the mark width.
  • Write the score plainly. "8 minutes, 3 tracks, 1 streak day."

Don't

  • No lime, no near-black neutrals, no neon glow. The old stadium look is retired.
  • No cream text on coral, rose or periwinkle. Contrast fails.
  • No gradients inside the mark or wordmark. Flat ink or flat coral only.
  • No music notes, crowns, vinyl-in-a-circle or leaderboard bars.
  • No emoji, no exclamation stacks, no fake urgency.
